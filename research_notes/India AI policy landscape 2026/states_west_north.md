# State-level AI policy instruments: Western, Central and Northern India (as of 19 November 2026)

Method note: WebSearch (first wave), then direct WebFetch of official portals, policy PDFs and news articles; Google News RSS used as a discovery substitute after the search budget was exhausted. Several publishers (ET, TOI, TNIE, ThePrint) block fetching, so some 2026 items rest on headlines plus one readable outlet. Confidence levels in the JSON reflect that. Jurisdiction codes follow ISO 3166-2:IN with "CT" for Chhattisgarh and "DH" for DNH&DD.

## Key Question 1: What AI policy instruments exist in each jurisdiction, and what is each state's "state of play"?

### Takeaway
Eleven of the sixteen jurisdictions now have a cabinet-approved AI-specific instrument (policy, mission or action plan): Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh and Haryana have adopted ones; Madhya Pradesh, Uttarakhand and J&K have announced missions without a published policy; Delhi, Punjab, Himachal, Ladakh, Chandigarh and DNH&DD rely on programmes, roadmaps or central IndiaAI schemes. The period June–November 2026 was unusually dense (Chhattisgarh AI Mission, UP-AIM, UP data-centre and startup policies, Gujarat data-centre GR, Haryana draft data policy, Goa AI Policy 2026 cabinet approval on 18 Nov 2026).

### Cited Findings

#### Maharashtra (MH) — state of play
Maharashtra has a dedicated, cabinet-approved AI Policy 2026 (29 April 2026) plus a sectoral MahaAgri-AI Policy 2025–29 (17 June 2025). A new data-centre policy was reported as being drafted in September 2026. No GR text for the AI policy was located online.
- Cabinet chaired by CM Fadnavis approved the AI Policy 2026 on 29 April 2026; targets ₹10,000 crore investment and 1.5 lakh jobs by 2031; six AI Centres of Excellence, five AI Innovation Cities, 2,000 GPUs on a "Compute as a Service" basis, 12 AI incubators, 2 lakh people trained, 5,000 MSMEs assisted, a State AI Data Exchange, a Maharashtra AI Mission, a Maharashtra Centre for Advanced AI Training, and an ethical-AI framework described as a first among states — [PSU Watch](https://psuwatch.com/newsupdates/maharashtra-cabinet-approves-ai-policy-2026); [News On AIR, 27 May 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/maharashtra-announces-ai-policy-2026-to-boost-industry-governance-and-public-services/) (adds ₹500 crore AI Startup Venture Fund, 20% subsidy on AI implementation cost for 5,000 MSMEs, Maha AI Tools Hub); [Indian Infrastructure](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/04/30/maharashtra-approves-ai-policy-with-rs-100-billion-investment-target/) (state contribution ₹250 crore to a ₹500 crore VC fund).
- MahaAgri-AI Policy 2025–29 approved by cabinet 17 June 2025; ₹500 crore for first three years; three-tier structure (State Steering Committee, State Technical Committee, an AI & Agritech Innovation Centre) and AI research centres at the four agricultural universities; builds on AgriStack, MahaVedh, CropSAP, Maha-DBT — [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/maharashtra-cabinet-approves-mahaagri-ai-2025-29-to-transform-agri-sector/); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/cabinet-nod-for-maharashtra-agriculture-artificial-intelligence-policy-2025-29/2661208/).
- A Maharashtra data-centre policy regulating sites, water use, noise and heat was reported as forthcoming on 7 Sept 2026 (Free Press Journal headline via Google News; article text not retrievable) — [Google News query](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Maharashtra+%22data+centre+policy%22+2026&hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en).

#### Gujarat (GJ) — state of play
Gujarat's primary AI instrument is the executive "Action Plan for Implementation of AI 2025–2030" approved by the CM on 27 July 2025 (not a gazetted policy). It is backed by the DST's policy stack: IT/ITeS Policy 2022–27 (AI CoE provision), GCC Policy 2025–30 (GR 11 Feb 2025, AI a named focus area), the Viksit Gujarat Data Center Policy 2026–29 (GR approved 6 Aug 2026, vision: "India's leading hyperscale AI data center destination"), and the Indian AI Research Organisation (IAIRO) at GIFT City (₹300 crore, operational 1 Jan 2026). No separate "Gujarat State AI Policy" or cabinet decision of Sept 2025 could be verified; the "Cabinet approved Sept 2025" starting point appears to conflate the July 2025 action plan.
- AI Action Plan 2025–2030: approved 27 July 2025; six pillars (data, digital infrastructure incl. GPU/AIRAWAT access, capacity building for 2.5 lakh people, R&D/use cases, startup facilitation, safe and reliable AI); 10-member AI Taskforce; a dedicated "AI and Deep Tech Mission"; AI CoE at GIFT City; AI factories in Tier-2/3 cities — [Outlook India (govt release)](https://www.outlookindia.com/announcements/gujarat-cm-bhupendra-patel-unveils-5-year-ai-action-plan-2025-2030-to-lead-ai-driven-governance-digital-innovation); [Elets eGov](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2025/07/ai-mission-2025-to-2030-announced-by-gujarat-chief-minister/).
- Viksit Gujarat Data Center Policy (2026-29): GR No. DST/MIT/e-file/24/2026/0624/IT, approved 6 Aug 2026; operative three years, in-principle approvals until 5 Aug 2029; EFCI ceiling ₹60,000 crore per 1 GW project; 2.5% capital subsidy (Dholera only), up to 4% interest subsidy for 10 years capped ₹25 crore/yr, ₹1/unit power tariff reimbursement for 20 years, 100% stamp duty, electricity duty (20 yrs) and SGST reimbursements, desalination plant support of 20% capex or ₹2 crore/MLD — [GR PDF](https://dstpolicy.gujarat.gov.in/public/assets/dcpolicy/Viksit_Gujarat_Data_Center_Policy_GR_(2026-29).pdf) (read directly).
- GCC Policy 2025–30: Resolution DST/ITM/e-file/24/2025/0173/IT dated 11 Feb 2025; targets 250 GCCs, 50,000 jobs, ₹10,000 crore; focus areas include "AI-driven digital transformation", data centres, cybersecurity; Operational Guidelines 1.0 issued 3 and 8 Oct 2026 — [GR PDF](https://dstpolicy.gujarat.gov.in/public/assets/gccpolicy/GCC_POLICY.pdf); [DST policy portal](https://dstpolicy.gujarat.gov.in/).
- IT/ITeS Policy 2022–27 (7 Feb 2022, amended 11 Oct 2024): 25% capex support (data centres up to ₹150 crore + ₹1/unit power subsidy), provision for a "Gujarat AI School / AI Centre of Excellence" — [Policy PDF with amendments](https://dstpolicy.gujarat.gov.in/public/assets/itpolicy/document/IT-POLICY-2022-27-Print-Version-With-All-Amedments.pdf); [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarat_IT/ITeS_policy_2022%E2%80%932027).
- IAIRO: ₹300 crore five-year outlay approved 30 Dec 2025, PPP model at GIFT City, operational 1 Jan 2026 — [Elets eGov, Nov 2026](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2026/11/why-gujarat-is-emerging-as-a-destination-for-ai-technology-and-innovation/); multiple outlets via [Google News query](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=%22Gujarat+Data+Centre+Policy%22+OR+%22IAIRO%22+Gujarat+AI+research+organisation&hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en).
- 37 Gujarat institutes identified for Data & AI Labs under the central IndiaAI Mission (ET Education, 5 Aug 2026) — central scheme, not a state instrument; noted only.

#### Rajasthan (RJ) — state of play
Rajasthan has a cabinet-approved Rajasthan AI/ML Policy 2026 (cabinet 31 Dec 2025, launched 6 Jan 2026) issued by DoIT&C, preceded by a public draft "Rajasthan AI Policy 2025" (Aug 2025). It sits on top of the Data Centre Policy 2025 (April 2025) and RIPS 2024. The Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Act 2023 remains the only state statute touching algorithmic management in the region.
- Cabinet 31 Dec 2025 approved the "Rajasthan Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI-ML) Policy-2026": AI Centre of Excellence; AI Nodal Officer in every department; top-up incentives aligned with RIPS and Startup Policy; AI skilling in schools/ITIs/polytechnics; requirement that AI systems be transparent, accountable, fair, privacy-protected, explainable and auditable; mechanism for AI-related cybercrime reporting — [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/rajasthan-cabinet-approves-ai-ml-policy-2026-promotes-ethical-and-responsible-ai/).
- Official portal hosts the final policy PDF, 10,000+ fellowships, GPU grants, a "Gov-AI Sandbox", State AI LMS (12,698 enrolments) and describes the policy as a top-up to RIPS 2024, MSME 2024, Data Centre 2025 and Startup 2022 — [ai.rajasthan.gov.in](https://ai.rajasthan.gov.in/).
- Launch 6 Jan 2026 at the Rajasthan Regional AI Impact Conference; GPU RFP for the State Data Centre issued 24 March 2026; 6,786 officers trained by Dec 2025 — [IMPRI](https://www.impriindia.com/insights/policy-update/rajasthan-ai-ml-policy-2026/).
- Draft Rajasthan AI Policy 2025 put out for public suggestions (Aug 2025) with a four-tier structure (AI Apex Committee, Steering Committee, AI Task Force, departmental nodal officers) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/rajasthan-govt-working-on-3-policies-on-ai-gaming-data-centres); [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/en/special-news/bhajnalal-government-to-launch-ai-policy-2025-benefits-for-colleges-startups-and-private-companies-19831818).
- Data Centre Policy 2025 (announced 12 April 2025; ₹20,000 crore target over five years; ₹10–20 crore annual asset-creation incentive for 10 years; 25% sunrise top-up for first three >₹100 crore data centres; 5% interest subsidy; 100% exemption of transmission/wheeling charges; 50% green incentive up to ₹12.5 crore) — [PSU Watch](https://psuwatch.com/newsupdates/rajasthan-data-centre-policy-to-attract-rs-20000-crore-investment-in-five-years-cm-bhajanlal-sharma).

#### Chhattisgarh (CT) — state of play
Chhattisgarh moved from infrastructure (AI Data Centre Park, Nava Raipur, May 2025) to an AI and digital-governance roadmap (3 July 2026) and a cabinet-approved ₹500 crore Chhattisgarh AI Mission (5 Aug 2026). Officials have proposed a dedicated state AI policy with DPDP-aligned safeguards; none is adopted yet.
- AI Mission: cabinet approval 5 Aug 2026; ₹500 crore over five years; 100 data labs; 7.5 lakh people trained; 300+ startups supported (six outlets concur) — [Google News query](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Chhattisgarh+cabinet+%22AI+Mission%22+500+crore&hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en).
- Roadmap 3 July 2026, Electronics & IT Dept: five pillars (AI skills, startups, awareness, safe and responsible AI, AI in governance); AI CoE, data labs and SOC in Nava Raipur — [Elets eGov](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2026/07/chhattisgarh-unveils-ai-and-digital-governance-roadmap-to-drive-innovation-and-public-services/).

#### Uttar Pradesh (UP) — state of play
UP has no "AI policy" document but adopted a mission in its place: the Uttar Pradesh AI Mission (UP-AIM), cabinet 9 Oct 2026, run as a society under the IT & Electronics Dept. 2026 also brought the Data Centre Policy 2026 and Startup Policy 2026 (cabinet 7 July 2026; AI/deep-tech startup incentives) and a first amendment to the IT & ITeS Policy 2022 (Aug 2026). Older instruments: AI City Lucknow (2025), AI Pragya (May 2025), Digital Media Policy 2024, Data Centre Policy 2021 (lapsed Jan 2026).
- UP-AIM: >100 use cases, 25–30 scaled; 100 AI startups; 5 lakh AI-fluent youth; 50,000 "forward deployed AI professionals"; 75 district AI innovation centres; UP AI Stack (AI-ready data, operating framework, departmental apps, secure data exchange, GPU compute); Apex Body, Governing Body, Executive Committee, CEO; Master System Integrator — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/uttar-pradesh/uttar-pradesh-yogi-adityanath-cabinet-approves-ai-mission-to-support-100-startups-and-train-5-lakh-youth).
- Data Centre Policy 2026 (2 GW, ₹2 lakh crore; replaces 2021 policy that ended Jan 2026) and Startup Policy 2026 (₹1,000 crore fund; AI/deep-tech prototype up to ₹20 lakh, seed up to ₹30 lakh, patient capital up to ₹100 crore, R&D support up to 40%; 20 CoEs at ₹12 crore each; Startup Mission Directorate) — [Convergence Now](https://www.convergence-now.com/policy-regulation/cm-yogi-approves-startup-data-centre-policy-to-boost-innovation-%E2%82%B92-lakh-cr-investment/) (gives 11 July 2026); ET Government/TOI/DQ India date the cabinet 7 July 2026 — [Google News query](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=%22Uttar+Pradesh+Startup+Policy+2026%22+OR+%22Data+Centre+Policy+2026%22+UP+cabinet&hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en).
- First amendment to IT & ITeS Policy 2022: capex subsidy 25% outside Noida/Ghaziabad, threshold ₹2 crore, co-working incentives up to ₹20 crore, >₹500 crore projects get 75% land rebate and up to 40% capex subsidy (cap ₹400 crore) — [Elets eGov, 31 Aug 2026](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2026/08/up-cabinet-approves-first-amendment-to-it-ites-policy/) (TOI reported the cabinet nod on 26 Aug 2026).
