## Does each state have a dedicated AI policy today, and what are its instruments?

### Takeaway
Tamil Nadu is the only southern state with a dedicated, still-operative AI policy (Safe & Ethical AI Policy 2020), now layered with a 2024 AI Mission that the new TVK government expanded and restructured in August 2026. Telangana governs through a 2020 AI Framework and a 2024 strategy document rather than a formal "policy"; Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh regulate AI mainly through adjacent instruments (gig-worker algorithm law, data-centre policy, robotics/physical-AI policy) with a Karnataka AI Policy announced but not published; Kerala folded a 10-year "Kerala AI Mission" into its IT Policy 2026 and created a cabinet AI portfolio in May 2026; Puducherry, Lakshadweep and A&N have no AI instrument.

### Cited Findings — Tamil Nadu (TN)
- The Tamil Nadu Safe & Ethical Artificial Intelligence Policy 2020 was issued by the Information Technology Department, prepared by the Commissionerate of e-Governance and TNeGA; it adopts the six-dimensional TAM-DEF evaluation framework (Transparency & Audit, Accountability & Legal issues, Misuse protection, Digital divide & data deficit, Ethics, Fairness & equity) and a seven-parameter DEEP-MAX scorecard, requires all government procuring agencies to consult CEET at TNeGA before AI procurement and to score solutions on DEEP-MAX (binary 0/1 initially, re-scored at most every 6 months), proposes an "ACTS" blockchain register of scores, and sets up a Safe & Ethical AI Monitoring Committee headed by the Chief Secretary; it applies to all state bodies, PSUs, boards, cooperatives and JVs of the state — [SPC policy page](https://spc.tn.gov.in/policy/tamil-nadu-safe-and-ethical-artificial-intelligence-policy-2020/); full text read from a mirrored copy [TN_Policy_for_Safe_and_Ethical_AI.pdf](https://www.tnebnet.org/awp/resource/TN_Policy_for_Safe_and_Ethical_AI.pdf)
- Tamil Nadu Data Policy 2022 (IT Department) sets a common framework for government databases, standards for classifying sensitive data, data quality and analytics for decision support, and sharing subject to privacy rules — [SPC policy page](https://spc.tn.gov.in/policy/tamil-nadu-data-policy-2022/)
- Tamil Nadu Data Centre Policy 2021 covered units incorporated 1 April 2021–31 March 2026 (now expired), ₹500 crore minimum investment, electricity-tax and open-access concessions, dual-grid supply above 50 MW, with no AI justification — [SPC page](https://spc.tn.gov.in/policy/tamil-nadu-data-centre-policy-2021); [Technodigital state guide](https://technodigital.in/data-center-policies-india-state-by-state-guide/)
- Tamil Nadu Artificial Intelligence Mission (TNAIM): GO reported 5 November 2024; ₹13 crore allocation; implemented by TNeGA with iTNT, ICT Academy and ELCOT; chaired by the Chief Minister; covers predictive policy making, government adoption, capacity building, start-up engagement and compute/storage infrastructure; aim to make TN a leading AI hub within five years — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/tn-govt-to-soon-implement-tamil-nadu-artificial-intelligence-mission-810098)

Marie Wilson, TVK government, 6 August 2026; some outlets date it 5 or 7 August): AI Industry Development Programme to train 5 lakh youth in AI by 2031 with IIT Madras; "Arivagam" AI and Innovation City (₹5 crore feasibility study; AI university, semiconductor test lab, incubators); TN SPARK AI education expanded from 5,000 to 7,600 government schools with tiered curriculum for classes VI–XII; TN AI Mission expanded to quantum, AVGC-XR and deep tech; Namma Arasu WhatsApp services expanded from 76 to 275; budget heads of ₹398 crore for AI, IT & Digital Services and ₹2,022 crore for Labour & Skill Development — [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/tamil-nadu/artificial-intelligence-rules-government-thought-process-1976971); [AI Front Page](https://aifront-page.com/tamil-nadu-budget-2026-ai-arivagam-deep-tech-ai-skilling/); Tamil LLM led by Tamil Virtual Academy — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/tn-budget-2026-new-large-language-model-in-the-making-to-push-tamil-into-artificial-intelligence-era)

- Policy Note 2026-27 of the (renamed) Ministry of Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology and Digital Services, Minister Dr. Kumar R., 26 August 2026: TNAIM expanded to AI, quantum, AVGC-XR and deep tech; ELCOT to set up a Tamil Nadu AI Centre of Excellence with TNeGA, TIDCO and IIT Madras; government AI systems to be assessed on TAM-DEF/DEEP-MAX before deployment; citizens affected by AI-assisted decisions to receive written explanations in Tamil or English and may request human review; TN-CERT figures (1,500+ vulnerabilities, 475+ incidents, 3,200+ officials trained) — [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/tamil-nadu/tn-pushes-ai-digital-governance-and-high-speed-connectivity-1982311)

### Inferences
- Tamil Nadu's 2020 policy is binding on state agencies as a procurement precondition in form, but there is no public evidence of DEEP-MAX scores being published or of the ACTS blockchain being operational; the August 2026 Policy Note treats it as live.
- Telangana's "AI policy" question has been open since November 2024; nothing published through November 2026 indicates a cabinet-approved AI Policy, so the 2024 strategy remains the governing document.
- Karnataka's gig-worker Act is the only binding algorithm-transparency statute in the region in force; Telangana's bill mirrors it but passage is unconfirmed.
- The TVK government in Tamil Nadu has continued and expanded the DMK-era AI mission rather than replacing it; the Kerala UDF government has so far acted through portfolio creation and budget lines rather than a new policy text.

## State of play (one paragraph per jurisdiction)
- **Tamil Nadu:** Dedicated AI policy since 2020 (TAM-DEF/DEEP-MAX, procurement rule, Chief Secretary committee), Data Policy 2022, AI Mission (GO Nov 2024, ₹13 crore) chaired by the CM. The TVK government (May 2026) renamed the department to include AI, expanded TNAIM to quantum/AVGC-XR/deep tech, announced a Section 8 TNAIM company and a commercial "Tamil Nadu AI Vision" company, an ELCOT AI CoE, Arivagam AI City (₹5 crore study), a 5-lakh AI skilling programme to 2031, TN SPARK AI in 7,600 schools, a Tamil LLM, and a citizen right to written explanation/human review for AI-assisted decisions. The Jan 2026 Sarvam Sovereign AI Park MoU predates the change of government. No revised AI policy text has been issued.
- **Telangana:** No cabinet-approved "AI Policy"; governance rests on the AI Framework 2020 (six pillars, T-AIM with NASSCOM, AI Ethics Group) and the AI-Powered Telangana Strategy (Sept 2024; targets for 2027; AI Advisory Council; 200-acre AI City; TGDeX launched July 2025). A gig-worker bill with algorithm-transparency duties was cabinet-approved Nov 2025 (passage unconfirmed). School AI education directed Feb 2026. A policy framework has been "under study" since Nov 2024.
- **Karnataka:** No AI policy text; a "Karnataka AI Policy" was announced 14 July 2026 by CM Shivakumar with an AI University, AI Hub (opened 17 Nov 2026 at IIIT-B, ₹35 crore), Class VI AI curriculum and two hyperscale data centres. Binding instruments are adjacent: the Platform-Based Gig Workers Act 2025 (automated decision-system transparency, 14-day notice, 1–5% welfare fee) and the Sustainable Data Centre Policy 2026–31 (1 GW, ₹1,300.45 crore, AI-ready capacity).
