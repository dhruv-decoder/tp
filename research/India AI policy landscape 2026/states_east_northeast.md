Method note: 36 entries were compiled into `states_east_northeast.json`. Web search quota was exhausted mid-task; later discovery used Google News RSS feeds, state portals and direct fetches. Several official portals were unreachable from this environment (eandit.odisha.gov.in and itassam.assam.gov.in did not resolve; state.bihar.gov.in/it failed TLS; itc.nagaland.gov.in did not resolve; pib.gov.in returned 403). Gaps are listed per question.

## Key Question 1: What AI policy instruments exist in each jurisdiction (title, issuer, date, status, provisions, sources)?

### Takeaway
Three states now have cabinet-approved dedicated AI policies (Odisha, May 2025; Bihar, Oct 2026) or an official draft under consultation (Jharkhand, July 2026; Sikkim, draft posted March 2026). The remaining eight jurisdictions rely on data policies, IT policies, centrally funded Centres of Excellence, budget lines or administrative guidelines; Mizoram's September 2026 AI-tool guidelines are the only binding AI-specific instrument in the North East.

#### Jharkhand (JH) - state of play
