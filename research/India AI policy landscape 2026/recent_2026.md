## Key question 1: What new instruments appeared in the window (central and state)?

### Takeaway
No binding central AI law or rule was finalised in the window; the main central movement was (a) MeitY publicly committing to a regulation-focused AI consultation paper (announced 8 Oct 2026, still unpublished on 19 Nov 2026), (b) a wave of regulator action (SC draft court-AI regulations, RBI draft model-risk guidance, SEBI/IRDAI/RBI frontier-AI cyber directives, CERT-In AI security guidelines, TRAI draft telecom-AI guidelines), and (c) a dense run of state instruments (Maharashtra GR, Jharkhand draft policy, Chhattisgarh and UP AI Missions, Bihar, Goa and Andhra Pradesh policies).
