## Q1. India AI Impact Summit (New Delhi, Feb 2026): New Delhi Declaration and outcomes

### Takeaway
India hosted the fourth summit in the Bletchley–Seoul–Paris series at Bharat Mandapam (16–21 Feb 2026); the non-binding "New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact" was adopted on 21 Feb 2026 with 85–88 endorsements at adoption and 91 by 24 Feb 2026, including both the US and China. Follow-on summits go to Geneva (June 2027) and the UAE (2028).

## Q2. Bletchley (2023), Seoul (2024), Paris (2025)

### Takeaway
India signed Bletchley (Nov 2023), endorsed the Seoul Ministerial Statement (22 May 2024) but was not a party to the 11-member Seoul Leaders' Declaration, and signed the Paris Statement (11 Feb 2025) as summit co-chair and founding member of the Public Interest AI Platform.

### Cited Findings
- Bletchley Declaration, 1–2 Nov 2023: India listed among 28 countries + EU; commitments to international cooperation on frontier AI risk, risk-based policies, transparency from private actors, and an inclusive network of scientific research on frontier AI safety — [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-safety-summit-2023-the-bletchley-declaration/the-bletchley-declaration-by-countries-attending-the-ai-safety-summit-1-2-november-2023)
- Seoul Declaration (21 May 2024) signatories are 10 countries + EU; India not listed — [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/seoul-declaration-for-safe-innovative-and-inclusive-ai-ai-seoul-summit-2024/seoul-declaration-for-safe-innovative-and-inclusive-ai-by-participants-attending-the-leaders-session-ai-seoul-summit-21-may-2024)
- Seoul Ministerial Statement (22 May 2024): India listed among 27 countries + EU; commitments to share best practices and evaluation datasets via AI safety institutes, identify severe-risk thresholds for frontier models, consider environmental footprint, promote AI education — [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/seoul-ministerial-statement-for-advancing-ai-safety-innovation-and-inclusivity-ai-seoul-summit-2024/seoul-ministerial-statement-for-advancing-ai-safety-innovation-and-inclusivity-ai-seoul-summit-2024)
- Paris "Statement on Inclusive and Sustainable AI for People and the Planet", 10–11 Feb 2025: India is signatory #26 of 63 countries + AU Commission + EU; six priorities (access/divides, trustworthy AI, innovation without market concentration, labour market, sustainability, international governance coordination); India is a founding member of the Public Interest AI Platform and Incubator — [elysee.fr](https://www.elysee.fr/en/emmanuel-macron/2025/02/11/statement-on-inclusive-and-sustainable-artificial-intelligence-for-people-and-the-planet)

### Inferences
- India's omission from the Seoul Leaders' Declaration reflects invitation scope (G7+Australia, Korea, Singapore) rather than a refusal; it joined the broader ministerial track the next day.
- Domestic follow-through: IndiaAI Safety Institute announced Jan 2025 (The Hindu, Feb 2025 coverage) fits the Seoul Ministerial commitment on safety institutes.

### Gaps
- India's co-chair role at Paris is from general knowledge; not confirmed on a fetched official page.

## Q7. Military AI: REAIM, US Political Declaration, UN LAWS resolutions and CCW GGE

### Takeaway
India has consistently stayed outside the voluntary military-AI declarations: it did not endorse the REAIM 2023 Call to Action, the 2024 Blueprint for Action, or the 2026 A Coruña "Pathways to Action" (43 endorsers, India absent), nor the US Political Declaration; it voted against UNGA resolution 78/241 on LAWS (Dec 2023), preferring the CCW GGE and a politically binding instrument.

### Cited Findings
